Our Story

AutoFixGeorgia started in 2009 as a two-lift family workshop on the edge of Tbilisi. Word spread, the queue grew — and so did we.

Today we run a modern service center with 12 lifts and 18 certified technicians, but the rule from day one has not changed: explain clearly, repair honestly and never return a car we would not drive ourselves.
